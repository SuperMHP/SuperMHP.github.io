I am currently a **Postdoctoral Research Fellow** at The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK) and a **Researcher** at the Shanghai Artificial Intelligence Laboratory. I received my **Ph.D.** from the School of Electrical and Computer Engineering (formerly *Electrical and Information Engineering*) at The University of Sydney (USYD), where I was fortunate to be supervised by [Prof. Wanli Ouyang](https://wlouyang.github.io/) and [Prof. Yonghui Li](https://profiles.sydney.edu.au/yonghui.li). Prior to my Ph.D., I obtained my **Master**'s degree in 2022 from the School of Information Science and Technology at the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), under the supervision of [Prof. Bin Liu](https://eeis.ustc.edu.cn/2016/0422/c2615a19449/page.htm).

Research Interests
======
**AI for Science**: Developing advanced AI algorithms to address fundamental challenges in natural sciences, with a primary focus on astronomy, astrophysics, and life sciences.

**Computer Vision**: Advancing representation learning and uncertainty quantification algorithms for robust natural image understanding.
